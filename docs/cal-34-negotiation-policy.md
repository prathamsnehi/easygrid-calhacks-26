# CAL-34: short budget-controlled negotiation

Approved demo scope, October 24, 2026. Supersedes the broader preparation note.

- Start at Theo's fixed recommendation. Monthly rent stays fixed.
- Permit one final offer: match a request within the smaller of the fixed 120%
  parcel cap and the remaining project budget; otherwise offer that maximum.
- Target 30–45 seconds. Default connected-call safety limit: 60 seconds.
- A clear agreement to the current offer is enough; no formal readback or payment
  logistics. A requested amount is not acceptance.
- Price objections can receive one increase. Clear access refusal or a request to
  stop ends immediately. Failed price negotiation stops the workflow.
- Unclear/conditional answers and technical failures need review. Every terminal
  result has a specific, factual explanation with amounts only when known.
- No alternative-route comparisons, automatic rerouting, or complex-condition
  negotiation. Theo's separate CAL-35 demonstration remains independent.
- Keep authoritative IDs/versions, exact cents, evidence checks, duplicate defense,
  atomic reservations and all-parcel agreement readiness.

Implementation and verification details are in [CAL-34 integration](CAL34_INTEGRATION.md).
